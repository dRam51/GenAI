# About Devin

Devin is a Cyber Infrastructure Engineer at Leidos, a leading defense contractor, where he supports the Advanced Battle Management System (ABMS) — a classified Air Force program focused on enabling the AF battle network. Throughout his career, he has contributed to a range of high-impact programs across the Air Force, and has held roles supporting the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA), U.S. Special Operations Command (SOCOM), and U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM). Devin has been in the defense industry since 2021, and holds a Bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity from the University of South Florida (2023) and a Master's degree in Cybersecurity from Western Governors University (2024).

As a defense professional, Devin is passionate about integrating AI into his programs to help counter nation-state adversaries who are increasingly leveraging AI to carry out sophisticated attacks. At the same time, he recognizes that AI is still an emerging technology that requires rigorous security practices — ensuring it cannot be weaponized or exploited against the systems and people it is meant to protect.

**Key areas where Generative AI can make an impact:**
- Defending against malicious actors by detecting and responding to AI-driven threats in real time
- Supporting offensive operations by leveraging AI to target and disrupt foreign adversaries
- Automating workflows and processes to increase operational efficiency across defense programs
